How it works

Two checks stand between the AI and your billing

The model can suggest anything. Only code decides whether money moves or a plan is cancelled, and it says no unless every rule passes.

Ticket

One message from one customer, with only that customer's records loaded.

AI proposal

A reply plus an action: answer, refund, cancel, or hand off.

Check 1Record check

Every invoice or plan it cites must exist and belong to this customer.

Check 2Rule check

$50 limit, 30-day window, paid invoices only, and the customer must clearly ask.

Done, or a person

Approved actions run and are logged. Everything else goes to a person with a review packet.
