Decision path

Six steps, two hard gates

The model can suggest an action, but it cannot bypass customer-record checks or policy rules.

01Ticket

Receive one request tied to one customer.

02Retrieve

Load only that customer's records.

03Propose

The model suggests an answer or action.

04 · GATEGrounding

Every cited record must exist and belong to the customer.

05 · GATEPolicy

Caps, windows, ownership, and allowed actions live in code.

06Resolve or review

Approve the action or send the reason to a person.

What this result means

The snapshot verifies a fixed test model, customer-record checks, and rules that reject anything not explicitly allowed.

  • It is not production traffic.
  • It does not claim a real model is 100% accurate.
  • It does show that failed gates do not approve a state-changing action.
